## One-glance cheat sheet {#cheat}

### Pathways in one line each

| Pathway | Chain | Off switch |

### Numbers worth knowing

- Over 700 GPCRs in humans. 21 Gα, 6 Gβ, 12 Gγ.
- Resting potential about -70 mV. Rod in dark about -30 mV.
- K+ 139 in / 4 out mM. Na+ 12 in / 145 out mM.
- ChR2 opens at about 470 nm (blue).
- More than 60 RTK genes, about 20 subfamilies. 115 SH2 proteins. Eph is the largest RTK family (13; 8 EphA, 5 EphB).
- HER1 surface half-life 10-15 h; endocytosis up 10x after EGF.
- Ras G12 mutation; Ras hyperactive in 30% of tumors.
- About 100 early response genes.
- About 800 ommatidia, 8 photoreceptors each; R7 recruited last.
- Carcinomas about 80% of cancers. Metastasis about 90% of cancer deaths.
- Tumors max about 2 mm without blood vessels.
- Fibroblasts stop after 25-50 doublings. Telomere repeat TTAGGG.
- IDH mutant in about 70% of certain brain cancers, about 25% of AML.
- Gleevec approved 2001.
- Rb on chromosome 13. Sporadic retinoblastoma about 1 in 30,000. 19 Wnts; 33 TGFβ superfamily members.
- Colon: APC over 80%, p53 60%, K-Ras 40%, Smad4 30%, MLH1 15%, TGFβRII 10%, β-catenin 5-10%.
- Smad2/Smad4 on 18q. FOP: ACVR1 R206H, about 1 in 2,000,000. About 1% of genes cancer-critical.

### Mix-ups to avoid

- GPCR (7 passes, no enzyme) vs RTK (1 pass, is a kinase).
- Trimeric G protein (Gαβγ) vs monomeric GTPase (Ras, Rho).
- GEF turns ON, GAP turns OFF.
- Muscarinic ACh receptor (GPCR, heart, slows) vs nicotinic ACh receptor (channel, skeletal muscle, contracts).
- Gβγ opens the heart K+ channel, not Gα.
- Closed Na+ channel can open; inactivated can't.
- Vision lowers cGMP; smell raises cAMP.
- ChR2 = channel (cations in, excite). HR = Cl- pump in (inhibit). BR = H+ pump out (inhibit).
- Phosphorylation can inhibit (glycogen synthase, c-Src Tyr527).
- Dominant negative = loss-of-function. Constitutively active = gain-of-function.
- Benign stays inside the basal lamina; malignant breaks through.
- MS hits CNS myelin; Guillain-Barré hits PNS myelin.
- Mutagen (DNA change, Ames) vs carcinogen (causes cancer). Indirect carcinogens are still genotoxic.
- Gene amplification = too much normal protein. Point mutation or fusion = abnormal hyperactive protein.
- Colon cancer order: APC, K-ras, Smad4 (chr 18q), p53. "A Kid Should Panic."
- Oncogene = one copy, dominant, hotspot mutations. Tumor suppressor = both copies, recessive, scattered truncating mutations.
- APC = tumor suppressor; β-catenin = oncogene (same pathway).
- Wnt's TCF (with β-catenin) is not the c-fos TCF (with SRF).
- β-catenin phosphorylation = destruction. Smad phosphorylation = NLS unmasked. c-Src Tyr527 phosphorylation = OFF.
- TGFβ receptors are Ser/Thr kinases (type II activates type I), not RTKs.
- TGFβ: suppressor early, pro-oncogenic late.

### Broken off switches (the theme of the whole midterm)

Cholera toxin (Gαs can't hydrolyze GTP), RasD (GAP can't act), sarin (AChE blocked), cocaine and SSRIs (reuptake blocked), VHL loss (HIF not degraded), c-Src without Tyr527 (self-lock gone), TPA (DAG that never leaves), constitutive EpoR (receptor always on), Bcr-Abl (kinase always on), APC loss (β-catenin never destroyed), ACVR1 R206H (BMP receptor always on).
